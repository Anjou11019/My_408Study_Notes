# $x\to0$
$$
e^x=1+x+\frac{x^2}{2!}+...+\frac{x^n}{n!}+o(x^n)
$$
$$
\begin{gather*}
\sin x=x-\frac{x^3}{3!}+...+(-1)^{n-1}\frac{x^{2n-1}}{(2n-1)!}+o(x^{2n})\\\\
\cos x = 1-\frac{x^2}{2!}+...+(-1)^n\frac{x^{2n}}{(2n)!}+o(x^{2n})\\\\
\ln(1+x)=x-\frac{x^2}{2}+...+(-1)^{n-1}\frac{x^n}{n}+o(x^n)\\\\
(1+x)^\alpha=1+\alpha x+\frac{\alpha(\alpha-1)}{2!}x^2+...+\frac{\alpha(\alpha-1)...(\alpha-n+1)}{n!}x^n+o(x^n)\\\\
\frac{1}{1-x}=1+x+x^2+...+x^n+o(x^n)\\\\
\frac{1}{1+x}=1-x+x^2+...+(-1)^nx^n+o(x^n)
\end{gather*}
$$
**常用的有限次展开**：
$$
\begin{gather*}
\arctan x = x-\frac{1}{3}x^3+\frac{1}{5}x^5+o(x^6)\\\\
\tan x=x+\frac{1}{3}x^3+o(x^3)\\\\
\arcsin x=x+\frac{1}{3!}x^3+o(x^3)
\end{gather*}
$$
